Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026. This policy covers health information in Life OS, including under Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar state laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy.

What health data we collect

Only what you or someone in your household chooses to add, for example: appointments and checkups, routines such as dental cleanings, medication or refill reminders, records and their dates, notes, and wellness logs like workouts. This may be about you or about family members you add, including children. We do not collect health data from any other source, and we do not infer health conditions from what you add.

Why we collect it

Only to provide Life OS to your household: to store it, show it in your calendar and lists, remind you about it, and include it in your morning email. We ask for your consent at sign-up before you can add any.

Who we share it with

No one except the service providers listed in our Privacy Policy, who process it only to run Life OS on our behalf. If you connect Google Calendar and Tasks, the items you choose (an appointment, say) are sent from your device to your own Google account, at your direction; you can untick appointments or disconnect at any time. We never sell health data and never use it for advertising.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we hold your health data, and get a copy: Settings → Your data → Export, or write to us.
  • Delete it: delete the items, or your account. Deletion reaches our encrypted backups within 30 days.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the data or your account.
  • Appeal: if we decline a request, reply to our answer to appeal, and we’ll respond within 45 days. If you’re still unhappy, you can contact your state’s attorney general.
