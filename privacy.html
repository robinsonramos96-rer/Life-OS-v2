Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026. Life OS is operated by [Your legal name or business name] (“we”). Questions: [support email].

The short version

  • We collect what you put into Life OS and what we need to run your account. Nothing else.
  • Only people in your household can see your household’s records.
  • We don’t sell or rent personal data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.

What we collect

Account information

Your name, email address and password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider), your household’s name and time zone, which household you belong to and your role in it, and the version of these terms and of the health-data consent you agreed to, with the date.

What you add

Tasks, events, routines, notes, logs, records, projects, goals, the things you track, and the people in your household (including children, whom you add as subjects). Some of this may be health information; see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Email you send to Life OS

When you send or forward an email or calendar invite to your household’s address, we read its sender, recipients, subject, text and any calendar attachment to create items. We keep the items it creates. We don’t keep the email itself after processing, apart from a short log entry (sender address, time, number of items made, outcome) used for security and rate limits. Depending on how your address is set up, mail passes through [Google (Gmail) or Postmark] first.

Google Calendar and Tasks, if you connect them

Each adult can choose to connect their own Google account. Google then gives your browser a short-lived access token with two permissions: to create and manage only the calendars Life OS itself creates, and to use Google Tasks. Google’s Tasks permission covers all your task lists; Life OS only reads and changes the one called “Life OS” that it makes. We also receive your Google email address, to show which account is connected.

The token stays in your browser and is never sent to our servers. Your device sends the items you chose (titles, dates, times, notes, domain and a link back to the item) to your Google account, and reads back the events and tasks in the Life OS calendar and list so changes you make there come back. Life OS stores, with your household’s data, the IDs of that calendar and list, your connected Google email, your choices, and a fingerprint of what was last sent for each item. It never reads your other calendars, task lists, email or contacts.

Life OS’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don’t use it for advertising, don’t sell it, don’t let people read it, and don’t use it to train AI models. Disconnect any time in Settings (Remove from Google deletes the Life OS calendar and list), or remove Life OS’s access in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services.

Billing

If you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive and store your Stripe customer and subscription references, plan status, billing interval and period dates. We never receive your full card number.

Device and technical data

The app stores your data on your device so it works offline, with a random device identifier and sync timestamps. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

How we use it

  • To provide Life OS to your household: storing, syncing, sorting and reminding.
  • To send the emails you expect: the morning email (which you can turn off), sign-in and password emails, invites, and billing notices.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To comply with law.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, each only to run Life OS:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in the United States.
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • GitHub: hosts the website and app files, and runs the nightly job that sends morning emails and makes encrypted backups.
  • Google: the Gmail account that sends the morning email [and relays Email to Life OS, if you use the Gmail relay]. If you connect Google Calendar and Tasks, your items go from your device to your own Google account, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • [Postmark: receives Email to Life OS, if you use it instead of Gmail]
  • [Cloudflare Turnstile: the “I am human” check on sign-up, if enabled]

We share personal data with others only if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a sale of the business (in which case this policy keeps applying to your data).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account erases your household’s records from the live database immediately if you are the owner (or removes you from the household if you are not). Encrypted backups are deleted automatically within 30 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • See and take your data: Logs & history → Download Excel, or Settings → Your data → Full backup.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app.
  • Delete it: delete individual items, or Settings → Sign out or delete → Delete.
  • Withdraw consent for health information by deleting it, or your account.
  • Stop the morning email: Settings → You and your time.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to know, access, correct, delete or port your data, and to appeal a decision we make about your request. Write to [support email]; we will respond within 45 days and won’t treat you differently for asking.

Children

Life OS is for adults. Accounts are for people 18 and over, and the service is not directed to children under 13. Parents may add information about their own children, and are responsible for it. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

See our Security page. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
