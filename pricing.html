How billing works

What happens at the end of the free trial?

If you added a card, your plan starts and the first charge happens then. If you didn’t, nothing is charged. Your data stays readable and exportable, and adding or changing things pauses until you choose a plan in Settings → Plan.

If I add a card early, do I lose free days?

No. The first charge waits until your 30 days are up.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Plan → Manage billing. You keep full use until the end of the period you paid for, and you’re not charged again.

Does my partner need to pay too?

No. One plan covers everyone in the household.

Do you see my card number?

No. Checkout and the billing page are Stripe’s. Life OS only keeps whether your plan is active and Stripe’s reference to it.
