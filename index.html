Who can see our data?

Only the people in your household. The database itself enforces this on every request, so one household can never read another’s records. We don’t sell data, show ads, or use it to train AI models.

What should I not put in it?

Card numbers, bank account numbers and ID numbers like Social Security numbers. Life OS doesn’t need them to remind you, so leave them where they are and write where to find them instead.

Does it replace my calendar?

No, it works with it. Connect Google Calendar and Tasks and Life OS keeps its own calendar and task list there, with reminders. Forward invites from any calendar to Life OS, or download a calendar file of everything for Apple or Outlook.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you choose a plan, nothing changes. If you don’t, your data stays readable and exportable, and adding or changing things pauses until you do.
